# The Field I Remember

**Track:** Track 2 — Commercial
**Final generation model:** `hy-image-v3.5-preview`, served through GMI Cloud

## Concept

This work begins with an ordinary place I know: a harvested field in my hometown on the North China Plain. The lone tree, village horizon, utility poles and transmission towers, and dark traces of burned corn stalks are not scenic decoration; they are the details that make the place recognizable as home.

The visual direction uses Impressionist language—broken color, softened edges, atmospheric light, and a cooler violet-blue shadow structure—to translate the photograph into memory without pretending the place was prettier than it was. It is not intended as a replica or imitation of any Claude Monet painting.

> AI did not invent a prettier hometown; it helped me see a known place differently.

## Workflow

1. **Original photograph.** I took the hometown photograph included as `source-photo.jpg`.
2. **Visual-language exploration.** Early tests included text-only prompting, reference-guided generation, and style-transfer experiments. Supporting AI tools helped explore visual language and organize the workflow; their exact model provenance is not fully documented, so it is recorded as **UNKNOWN** rather than attributed to the final model.
3. **Reference-guided final generation.** The final image was generated by `hy-image-v3.5-preview` through GMI Cloud. The request used two references with deliberately separate roles:
   - a composition/content reference derived from the hometown-photo exploration;
   - a public-domain museum image of Claude Monet's *The Houses of Parliament, Sunset* as a style-only reference.
4. **Organization and provenance.** Codex organized the experiments, API calls, artifact checks, and provenance records. Helper tools and models did not replace the required final generation model.
5. **Final selection.** The selected Round 04 output was copied byte-for-byte to `final.png`. It received no retouching, repainting, upscaling, color adjustment, crop, or other visual edit for submission.

## What remained real

- the harvested North China Plain farmland;
- the central lone tree and low village horizon;
- the utility poles and transmission towers;
- the dark burned corn-stalk traces in the foreground;
- the vertical viewpoint and path through the field.

## Public-release note

`source-photo.jpg` is included intentionally as the entrant's source photograph. A public-release audit found no visible people, address, license plate, or direct personal identifier, and no GPS, capture-time, device, or author metadata. The photograph still shows ordinary rural infrastructure and landscape context; publishing that context is part of the entrant's submission decision.
